Privacy notice

Internal staff tools — ordering (IOS), recipe costing (RCC) and booking. Last updated 22 September 2026.

What these apps are

The applications hosted under order.hideoutdb.com, hideout-recipe-cost.web.app and the connected Google Apps Script backends are internal tools used by staff of The Hideout Specialty Coffee (Brisbane, Australia) to run the cafés: placing supplier orders, tracking stock, costing recipes and managing bookings. They are not offered to the general public.

What we store

  • Staff sign-in: the Google account email address of a staff member who signs in, and the role and stores assigned to them.
  • Operational data: orders, stock counts, recipes, ingredient prices, supplier invoice lines, prep and closing checklists, staff notices and booking details entered by staff.
  • Customer booking details where a booking is recorded: name, contact details and the booking itself.

We do not collect payment card details in these apps.

Where it lives

Data is stored in Google Cloud services operated for The Hideout — Google Firestore, Google Apps Script and Google Sheets — under accounts controlled by the business. Supplier invoice emails are read from the business mailbox to update ingredient prices.

How it is used

Only to operate the business: ordering, stock, costing, rostering and bookings. We do not sell or share this data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google account access

When a staff member signs in with Google, the apps request only what is needed to identify the person (email address) and, for the backend automation that runs under the business account, access to that business account's Gmail, Drive and Sheets to process supplier invoices, bookings and reports. This access is used by the business's own automation and is not shared.

Retention and removal

Operational records are kept while they are needed to run the business. A staff member's access is removed when they leave. To ask about or remove personal information, contact us below.
